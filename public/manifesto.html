01 — The problem

Your AI has no memory of its own. Every session starts from zero.

The most powerful tool ever built for knowledge workers is re-learning your world every single time you open it. Its memory is a folder of uploaded files, a project someone remembered to attach, a context window that forgets. There is no durable brain.

Meanwhile your real knowledge lives scattered across Salesforce, Outreach, HubSpot — account states, renewal risk, pricing authority, the actual reasons deals happen. Your AI can't see any of it, and when it works, it works on temporary context, not durable truth.

So we built the missing layer. Not another chat UI. Not another lock-in. A brain.

02 — The split

Two things must never be merged: your knowledge and your harness.

Your LLM is a commodity you rent by the token. Your knowledge is intellectual property you own for life. Sell the first if you must — never lease the second. Sabo is built on that line.

Your knowledge — the brain

Plain OKF markdown, in your control. One concept per file, the path is the identity, cross-linked, human- and agent-readable. Versioned, audited, encrypted where it matters. Portable to any tool, forever.

portable · no lock-in · yours

Your harness — the hands

Claude, GPT, an open-weight model, a fine-tune, a custom agent — your call, your key, your bill. It plugs into Sabo over an open protocol and gets your brain, pointed at your outcomes.

switchable · no commitment · theirs sure
03 — The manifesto

We believe:

/1

The brain and the harness are separate.

Vendor lock-in on the tool you think with is the most expensive tax in software. Your knowledge stays yours; your model stays yours.

/2

Open source is the trust story.

Procurement can read the audit log, the encryption, the key handling — because they're all public. We don't ask you to trust us; we show you the code.

/3

Plain markdown is the only format that lasts.

Every proprietary knowledge format is a hostage situation waiting to happen. Portable, readable, greppable — your corpus outlives every vendor.

/4

Accounts without outcomes are just files.

Your AI isn't there to answer trivia. It's pointed at north stars — renewals, expansions, activations — and every byte in the corpus serves those.

/5

The workspace describes itself.

No onboarding docs. Every LLM that connects explores the corpus structure itself and writes a map for the next session. No manual context handoff, ever.

/6

Agents can write — but they can never erase.

Full version history, immutable audit trail, soft deletes. Your AI's mistakes are recoverable, reviewable, and attributable. Trust, enforced by design.

/7

Flat infrastructure. Never tokens.

You bring the LLM and pay your provider directly. Sabo charges for the knowledge layer — the workspace, connectors, security, audit — not for the privilege of using your own model.

04 — The promise
The harness is the customer's. The outcomes are yours. Sabo is the brain.

Bring your own brain. Bring your own harness. Keep your own data. Sabo sits in the middle, making the two meet — turning scattered tool gravity into a durable corpus, and a durable corpus into outcomes.
