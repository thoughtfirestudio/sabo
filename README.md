# Sabo — the open-source brain for your AI, pointed at outcomes

_Sabo_ (yo sabo — "I know"). Teams keep a corpus of plain markdown — the
knowledge, the context, the why behind every decision. Any LLM of your choice
connects over MCP and grounds itself on that corpus, pointed at the metrics
that actually matter.

The brain and the harness are separate. Forever.

**Status:** the self-hostable `sabo` MCP server lands here at launch. The
manifesto below is what we're building toward.

## The split

Two things must never be merged: your knowledge and your harness.

Your LLM is a commodity you rent by the token. Your knowledge is intellectual
property you own for life. Sell the first if you must — never lease the second.

Your knowledge — the brain → Plain OKF markdown, in your control. One concept
per file, the path is the identity, cross-linked, human- and agent-readable.
Versioned, audited, encrypted where it matters. Portable to any tool, forever.

Your harness — the hands → Claude, GPT, an open-weight model, a fine-tune, a
custom agent — your call, your key, your bill. It plugs into Sabo over an open
protocol and gets your brain, pointed at your outcomes.

## The manifesto

1. **The brain and the harness are separate.** Vendor lock-in on the tool you
   think with is the most expensive tax in software. Your knowledge stays
   yours; your model stays yours.
2. **Open source is the trust story.** Procurement can read the audit log, the
   encryption, the key handling — because they're all public. We don't ask you
   to trust us; we show you the code.
3. **Plain markdown is the only format that lasts.** Every proprietary
   knowledge format is a hostage situation waiting to happen. Portable,
   readable, greppable — your corpus outlives every vendor.
4. **Accounts without outcomes are just files.** Your AI isn't there to answer
   trivia. It's pointed at north stars — renewals, expansions, activations —
   and every byte in the corpus serves those.
5. **The workspace describes itself.** No onboarding docs. Every LLM that
   connects explores the corpus structure itself and writes a map for the next
   session. No manual context handoff, ever.
6. **Agents can write — but they can never erase.** Full version history,
   immutable audit trail, soft deletes. Your AI's mistakes are recoverable,
   reviewable, and attributable. Trust, enforced by design.
7. **Flat infrastructure. Never tokens.** You bring the LLM and pay your
   provider directly. Sabo charges for the knowledge layer — the workspace,
   connectors, security, audit — not for the privilege of using your own
   model.
